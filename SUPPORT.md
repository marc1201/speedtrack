# Support

Speedtrack is an iPhone app that shows how fast you are moving and where you are on a map.

## Common questions

- **The speed shows "--".** The speed comes from GPS, and only while you move. Indoors or standing
  still there is often none yet, and the line under the number says why. Outdoors and moving, it
  shows within seconds. In min/km and min/mi, standing still always shows "--", because there is
  no pace. Once a speed has been measured, the last one stays on screen in grey until a new one
  arrives.
- **"Turn on Precise Location in Settings."** The speed needs precise location. In the iPhone's
  Settings, open Privacy & Security, then Location Services, then Speedtrack, and turn on Precise
  Location.
- **Which map?** Settings in the app chooses it. Apple Maps needs the internet. The outlines are
  built into the app. OpenStreetMap shows the countries you have downloaded, and they work without
  internet.
- **A download stopped.** Tap Download again, and it continues where it stopped. Downloads do not
  run while Low Data Mode is on.
